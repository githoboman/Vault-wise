BITCREDIT PITCH DECK
"Borrow USD. Keep Your Bitcoin."
=========================================================

1. THE PROBLEM
Bitcoin is the most valuable and widely held asset in crypto, yet the vast majority of it sits idle in cold storage. 
When Bitcoin holders need liquidity (cash) for real-world expenses or new investments, they are forced to sell their BTC. 
Selling triggers taxable events, incurs exchange fees, and eliminates their exposure to Bitcoin's future price appreciation.

2. THE SOLUTION
BitCredit is a fully decentralized, EVM-compatible lending protocol built natively on the BOT Chain.
We allow users to deposit their Wrapped Bitcoin (or equivalent bridged BTC) into a non-custodial smart contract to instantly mint a credit line.
Users can borrow USDC stablecoins against their Bitcoin collateral without ever selling it—completely tax-free.

3. WHY BOT CHAIN?
- High Performance & Low Fees: BitCredit requires instant, cheap transactions to manage liquidations and price oracle updates. BOT Chain provides the perfect EVM execution environment.
- Liquidity Sink: BitCredit will act as a massive "black hole" for Bitcoin liquidity, directly driving Total Value Locked (TVL) onto the BOT Chain network.
- Gas Consumption: Every deposit, borrow, and repayment action burns BOT for gas, creating constant, organic demand for the native token.

4. CORE PROTOCOL MECHANICS
- Over-Collateralization: The protocol enforces a strict 70% Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio. For every $10,000 of BTC deposited, a user can borrow a maximum of $7,000.
- Instant Settlements: No slow cross-chain bridges. Once the BTC is on BOT Chain, borrowing and repaying happens in seconds.
- Trustless & Transparent: All collateral is held in audited smart contracts.

5. BUSINESS & REVENUE MODEL
BitCredit is designed to be financially sustainable from day one:
- Origination Fee: The protocol charges a hardcoded 1% fee on all new loans. If a user borrows 1,000 USDC, the protocol keeps 10 USDC as revenue.
- Liquidation Penalties: In the event of a market crash, undercollateralized loans are liquidated with a built-in penalty fee that goes to the protocol treasury.
- Ecosystem Alignment: Revenue can be shared with the BOT Chain DAO or used to buy back BOT tokens.

6. THE INNOVATION: ON-CHAIN REPUTATION
BitCredit introduces a native Web3 Credit Score. 
As users borrow and reliably repay their debt, their immutable on-chain credit score increases (+1 point per $1 repaid, capped at 850). 
In the future, high-score users will unlock VIP perks:
- Lower origination fees
- Higher LTV ratios (undercollateralized lending)
- Ecosystem-wide identity verification
